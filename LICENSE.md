## 9. Practical Intent

The practical intent of this licensing model is simple:

- code should be easy to reuse under MIT;
- the framework ideas, documents, specifications and examples should be as free as possible under CC0;
- others should be able to fork, implement, adapt, criticise, commercialise or continue the work without needing permission.

The Thought Tree Framework is being released openly so that the idea can realise its potential beyond the original author’s capacity to continue developing it.
